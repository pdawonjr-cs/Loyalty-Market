Privacy policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This page explains what LocalMarket Liberia stores when someone submits a vendor application or an order request. Order requests do not take payment or guarantee stock or delivery.

Information we receive

A vendor application includes a business name, contact name, phone number, category, community, and business description. An order request includes a name, phone number, delivery area, payment preference, selected products and quantities, and total. We use these details to review applications and manually confirm order requests.

How information is stored and used

Submitted details are stored in the LocalMarket server database and are available to authorized marketplace administrators. The app does not process payments, arrange delivery, or send customer details to payment, advertising, analytics, or delivery providers. The server temporarily uses the request source IP address in memory to limit repeated failed administrator sign-ins. The hosting provider may process technical request logs under its own terms.

How long we keep information

Order requests are automatically deleted after {{DATA_RETENTION_DAYS}}. Vendor applications that are pending or declined are automatically deleted after {{DATA_RETENTION_DAYS}} from submission or the decline decision. Business contact details for verified vendors are kept while their vendor relationship is active; when it ends, those details are eligible for deletion under the same schedule. The automated cleanup runs once a day. The operator must apply the same limit to database backups.

Your choices

You can ask to access or delete information you submitted by contacting the privacy address below. Vendor applications and order requests are optional. Do not include payment credentials or other sensitive financial information.

Security

The service uses HTTPS in production, stores administrator passwords as salted scrypt hashes, and protects administrator sessions with short-lived, HTTP-only cookies. Access to the server and its backups is restricted to the marketplace operator.

Children

LocalMarket is intended for general audiences and is not designed specifically for children.
