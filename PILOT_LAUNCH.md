# LocalMarket first-user pilot

## Start with vendors

Begin in one community in Greater Monrovia, which is the area the current demo content depicts. Keep the service in preview mode while recruiting. Speak directly with a small first group of vendors and explain that LocalMarket is testing a catalog and order-request process: there are no online payments, seller alerts, or delivery service.

For each vendor, get permission to publish the business name, contact channel, product details, prices, and product photos. Review the business and contact information before marking the vendor verified. Ask each vendor to check stock and prices regularly and assign an operator to relay order requests.

## Bring in the first shoppers

After approved listings are live, invite shoppers through the vendors' own WhatsApp status, Facebook pages, existing customer groups, and in-person QR flyers at participating stalls. Start with direct invitations and referrals so the team can handle every request manually. Do not pay for ads or describe the marketplace as a delivery or payment service until those services exist.

## Weekly measures

- Vendors contacted, applications received, vendors approved, and live listings published.
- Visitors who view a listing and send an order request.
- Requests the team confirms with both sides, requests fulfilled, and requests cancelled.
- Vendor and shopper feedback, especially missing products, stale prices, slow replies, and confusing pickup/delivery expectations.
- Repeat shoppers and vendor referrals.

Use those results to decide whether to add more vendors in the first community before expanding to another area. A useful pilot goal is a handful of responsive vendors and enough real requests to observe the manual confirmation process; avoid treating sample listings, page views, or unconfirmed requests as marketplace traction.

## Invitation copy

**Vendor invitation**

> We are inviting a small group of local businesses to try LocalMarket Liberia, a new online catalog for local products. The pilot is free to try. Customers can send an order request, then you and the LocalMarket team confirm stock, payment, and delivery by phone. There is no online payment or automatic delivery. Would you like to review the pilot and decide whether we can list a few of your products?

**Shopper invitation**

> LocalMarket Liberia is testing a small catalog of products from participating local vendors. Browse the listings and send a request only if you are interested. The team will confirm stock, payment, and delivery with you by phone; no payment is taken on the site. We would appreciate your feedback after you try it.
